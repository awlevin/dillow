# What's new in Dillow

Notes on what's changed. Newest first.

## July 6, 2026

### 🐛 Bug fixes
- **The slider drags smoothly again on phones.** It was snapping back toward the middle while you dragged — that's fixed, so your guess follows your finger.

## July 1, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **Clues unlock as you guess.** The listing's secrets now come out one guess at a time: home facts (lot size, stories, garage) after your first, the listing description after your second, and the full feature list after your third. The ⓘ details modal shows what's still locked.
- **Photo gallery view.** Tap the grid button on the photos (or in the full-screen viewer) to see every shot at once and jump straight to the one you want — no more swiping through 30 photos.
- **Pinch to zoom.** In the full-screen photo viewer you can now pinch (or double-tap) to zoom in and pan around — inspect that countertop up close.
- **You vs. the crowd vs. the price.** The community verdict is now a scale showing exactly where your guess, the crowd's typical guess, and the asking price all landed.
- **Report a home.** Spot fake renovation renders or busted data? There's now a "Report a problem with this home" link on the reveal and in the listing details.
- **Recent homes.** A quiet "Recent homes" link at the bottom of the page lists the homes you've played — with the price, how close you got, and a jump to the real listing.

### 💅 Nits
- **Fine-tune the slider on your phone.** While dragging, slide your finger up or down away from the slider for finer control — like scrubbing a video.
- **Share your whole day at once.** The end-of-day recap now has a "Share results" button that sums up all three homes in one spoiler-free message.
- **How close was that guess?** Tap the ⓘ next to any past guess to see exactly how close it landed.
- **Your stats, by city.** The stats panel now toggles between accuracy by price and accuracy by city, so you can see where you've got the local touch.
- **Confetti in rooms too.** Nail a challenge-room home within 1% and you'll get the same celebration as the daily game.
- **"Your rooms" is now "Friends"**, and each room shows how many people have played today.
- Tap any gallery photo to open it full-screen, and the mobile map now has a clear "Done" button to step back to the photos.
- Full-screen photos ditch the 30-dot row for a clean progress bar, and the photo counter stays out of the way on mobile while you browse.
- Modals no longer overflow the screen on iOS.

## June 30, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **Three homes a day.** Instead of one, you now get three: the home of the day, a big-spender splurge from the top of the market, and one near you (we pick the closest city automatically — no permission prompt). Play through all three, then see your daily wrap-up.
- **A leaderboard for every home.** Each home keeps its own all-time board of the closest guesses ever made on it — so you're now racing everyone who's ever played that exact home, not just today's crowd. Land near the top and you'll stick around.

### 💅 Nits
- **Fuller photo galleries.** Homes now show their whole photo set — up to dozens of shots instead of a handful — so you've got more to study before you guess.
- **Jump back into a room.** A quiet "Your rooms" link on the home page reopens any challenge room you've recently played — tap to expand the list.
- **Verdicts that mean it.** Only a guess within 1% earns the 🎯 "Nailed it" now — every other result gets an honest read on how you did, from "so close" to a friendly roast.

## June 29, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **See how the crowd values it.** After you guess, find out whether Dillow players think a home is overpriced or a steal — based on everyone's first guesses.
- **See it on realtor.com or Zillow.** Once you've finished, jump straight to the real listing to browse the full details.
- **Full-screen map.** Open the location map full-screen to explore the neighborhood — handy on mobile — and every daily home now always comes with a map.

### 💅 Nits
- The ⓘ listing details and bigger photo galleries now show in challenge rooms too, not just the daily home.
- Cleaner number formatting in the listing details.

## June 26, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **Listing details.** Tap the info icon next to a home's title to dig into the full listing — description, features, lot size, schools, and more. (Anything that'd give away the price stays hidden until you finish.)

### 🐛 Bug fixes
- Homes no longer show up half-empty — every daily home now always includes the key stats: price, beds, baths, and square footage.

## June 25, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **Challenge Rooms, now for everyone.** Finish the daily home, then start a lightning round against a friend or a group: the same 3 homes, a live leaderboard, and round-by-round history. One tap to share, and you can rename your room.
- **Wider warmth bands.** A wildly-off guess now reads differently from a near-miss, with a new ❌ for the way-out ones.
- **Full-screen photos.** Tap any photo to open a swipeable full-screen viewer (with arrow buttons on desktop).

### 🐛 Bug fixes
- The leaderboard now ranks by your closest guess — fewer tries breaks ties — so the order always matches the "% off" you see.

### 💅 Nits
- A little house lights up room by room while listings load, and each photo develops into focus as it arrives.
- The "next home drops in" countdown now waits at the bottom of the page until you've taken in your result.

## June 24, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **Explore the map.** The location rides along as the last slide in the photo carousel — tap the home's title or the pin to open an interactive map.
- **Faster guessing.** Type an exact price with K / M shortcuts (type "250" then K), and decimals work too ("1.2" + M).
- **A live leaderboard.** Today's board updates as you play, with your own placement highlighted, while the answer stays hidden until the round ends.

### 🐛 Bug fixes
- The price slider now stretches to fit both the priciest and the cheapest homes, so today's price is never out of range.

### 💅 Nits
- A cleaner, tighter guess card so the Guess button sits right where you need it.

## June 23, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **Next-home countdown.** A live countdown to the next drop, plus a teaser of which city you're headed to. New homes now arrive at midnight Eastern.
- **More delight on every guess.** A hot/cold warmth burst, a confetti shower when you win, and the final price counting up slot-machine style on the reveal.

## June 22, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **Dillow is live!** Guess a daily home's listing price with an over/under slider — 5 tries, with higher/lower and warmth-band hints along the way.
- Swipeable photo galleries with extra interior shots and the year the home was built.
- A global leaderboard — add your name after you finish — plus your own stats over time and a play streak.
- Share your result to your phone's share sheet or clipboard, grid and all.
- A one-time How-to-Play walkthrough on your first visit (reopen it anytime with the "?").
- An optional map peek with an exact pin once the game's over.
